Grab A Cart India · Terms & Policies

Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 Compliance

1. Indian Rupee (₹ INR) Pricing & Taxes

All product prices are quoted in Indian Rupees (₹ INR) inclusive of GST (Goods and Services Tax). A full GST-compliant tax invoice is provided with every order.

2. Pan-India Delivery & Pincode Policy

We deliver across all 19,000+ Indian Postal Index Numbers (PIN codes) using Blue Dart, Delhivery, and express courier partners. For addresses outside India, our international shipping wing will be launching soon.

3. 7-Day Doorstep Returns & Refund Guarantee

Enjoy a 7-day doorstep return or replacement window from the delivery date for defective, damaged, or mismatched products. Refunds are initiated to the original UPI / Card / Bank account within 24-48 hours of pickup.

4. User Account Security & 2FA OTP

Users are responsible for keeping credentials secure. Login and registration require one-time password (OTP) verification to prevent unauthorized access.

5. Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Your personal information (name, address, contact details) is strictly secured with 256-bit encryption and is never sold to third parties. It is exclusively used for order fulfillment and customer service.
