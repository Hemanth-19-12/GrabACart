Grab A Cart Terms & Policies

E-Commerce Guidelines & Consumer Protection Compliance

1. Merchant Agreement & Code of Conduct

All sellers on Grab A Cart India must be registered business entities with a valid GSTIN and PAN. Merchants guarantee that all listed goods are 100% genuine, new, and compliant with BIS and Indian regulatory standards.

2. Fair Pricing & Price Transparency

All product prices are quoted in Indian Rupees (₹ INR) inclusive of all applicable taxes. Maximum Retail Price (MRP) must match manufacturer declarations. Artificial price inflation is strictly prohibited.

3. Order Fulfillment & 7-Day Returns

Orders must be packed and dispatched through approved logistics partners within 24 hours of placement. Consumers are entitled to a hassle-free 7-day doorstep replacement or refund for damaged or defective products.

4. Privacy & Data Protection

Customer data (names, phone numbers, addresses) is strictly confidential and protected by 256-bit encryption. It may only be used for direct order fulfillment and courier delivery.
